# Deposits and withdrawals

## Deposit

1. Choose **Deposit** in the app or Portfolio.
2. Read the instructions for your one-time deposit address.
3. Choose the asset and the network you are sending from.
4. Check the minimum deposit, estimated amount you will receive, and any deadline.
5. Send the correct asset on the selected network. Include a memo or reference if the instructions require one.
6. Follow the deposit's progress in **Transfers**.

Each generated deposit address is for one deposit only. Do not reuse it, save it for future deposits, share it, or send a small test payment first. Send at least the minimum shown in a single transfer.

An exchange may take time to send your withdrawal. Make sure it can send the correct asset and meet the deposit deadline.

## Withdraw

Choose **Withdraw** in the wallet or Portfolio. Choose the asset and network you want to receive, then enter the recipient's address. Check the address, network, amount, and minimum received before approving with your passkey.

Some destinations have extra requirements. If you withdraw wrapped NEAR, check that the receiving wallet or service accepts wrapped NEAR (`wrap.near`). Follow the requirements shown in the app.

## Check a transfer

The **Transfers** tab shows your activity and any transaction links. A transfer can be confirmed on the network you sent from before it reaches your {DYA_BRAND.name} balance.

If the result is unclear, check the existing transfer before trying again. An error message does not necessarily mean nothing was sent.
