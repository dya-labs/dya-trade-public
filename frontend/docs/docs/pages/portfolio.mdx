# Portfolio

Open **Portfolio** to see your assets and activity in one place.

## Current value

- **In wallet** is the estimated value of the assets in your wallet.
- **In open orders** is the estimated value of funds still held for your open orders, after completed trades.
- **Portfolio value** adds these two amounts together.

Deposits, withdrawals, and trades that are still being completed are not included. The information icon beside Portfolio value explains what is counted. A notice appears when prices are delayed or funds are still being processed.

Values are estimates based on available market prices. If an asset has no price, the total is incomplete; that does not mean the asset is worth nothing.

## Holdings

The table shows how much you can use, how much is held in orders, and your total for each asset. It also shows the price in USD, estimated value, and each asset's share of your portfolio.

Assets on different networks appear separately. Percentage shares are hidden when the total value is incomplete.

Portfolio shows what you hold now. It does not yet show past portfolio values or how much you have gained or lost.

## Activity

The activity tabs show orders, completed trades, and transfers across all markets. Open a row to see more details and any links to the transaction.
