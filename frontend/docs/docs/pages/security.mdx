# Security and account recovery

## Protect your passkey

Your passkey lets you sign in and approve actions. Keep access to the device or password manager that holds it, and check its backup and recovery options. {DYA_BRAND.name} cannot recreate a lost passkey for you.

Check that you are on the correct {DYA_BRAND.name} or {DYA_BRAND.name} ID website before approving a request. Read what you are approving each time.

## Set up recovery before you need it

Check **Account settings** in {DYA_BRAND.name} ID to see which recovery options are available for your account.

A recovery wallet must be added and verified before it can help you recover your account. Connecting a new wallet after losing your passkey is not enough to prove the account is yours.

If a backup or recovery setup says **Pending**, it is not ready to use yet.

Recovery has been tested on new accounts without funds. Recovery for accounts holding funds has not yet been fully verified. Keep your original passkey safe and do not rely on recovery as your only protection.

## If recovery is interrupted

Keep any new passkey created during recovery. If a step is waiting for confirmation, resume the same recovery process and check its status. Do not delete your passkeys or start over while a change is still pending.

Never give anyone your passkey, recovery-wallet keys, or sign-in details, even if they claim to be support. Only approve requests you understand and started yourself.
