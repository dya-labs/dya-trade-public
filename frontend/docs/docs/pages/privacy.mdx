# Confidential trading

{DYA_BRAND.name} uses NEAR's privacy technology to protect supported wallet balances and activity. Sign in to view your confidential balances, and confirm with your passkey when asked.

## Confidential does not mean anonymous

Privacy does not make every part of a transfer invisible. When you deposit or withdraw through a public network, addresses, amounts, and times may be visible there. The services handling a transfer also need some information to complete it.

{DYA_BRAND.name} keeps records needed to sign you in, track orders, and help with recovery.

The public trades shown on the chart are separate from confidential trades. Your confidential trades are not included in that public chart view.

## Your device

Sign out when using a shared device, and keep the device or password manager that holds your passkey secure.

{DYA_BRAND.name} remembers some settings in your browser, but does not save a copy of your private portfolio there. Saved settings cannot approve trades or move funds.
