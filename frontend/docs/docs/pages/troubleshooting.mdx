# Troubleshooting

## Your balances are locked

Follow the prompt to unlock your balances and confirm with your passkey through {DYA_BRAND.name} ID. On a different device, use the device or password manager where your existing passkey is saved.

## Prices or balances are delayed

Check any notices in the app and choose **Retry** if available. A problem with chart prices does not necessarily mean there is a problem with your account or trade.

Portfolio may continue showing the last available values if it cannot update. Read any notice before relying on those amounts.

## A transfer or order is pending

Find it in **Transfers**, **Open orders**, or **Order history**. Open its details to check the status and any transaction links. One part of a transfer or trade may finish before your balance updates.

Do not reuse a deposit address or approve the same action again just because the app stopped waiting for a response.

## No chart is available

Some tokens do not have a chart available yet. You may still see token information or be able to trade. Check the price and availability shown when reviewing your order.

## Reporting a problem

Keep the error reference, approximate time, asset, network, and any transaction link. Share these only through a support channel you know is genuine.

Never share your passkey, wallet recovery keys, or sign-in details. Avoid posting private account information publicly.
