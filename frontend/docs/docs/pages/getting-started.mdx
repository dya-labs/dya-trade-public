# Getting started

## Connect a wallet

Open <a href={DYA_URLS.app}>{DYA_BRAND.name}</a> and choose **Connect**. Follow the steps in {DYA_BRAND.name} ID to create an account or sign in with your passkey.

Your passkey is saved by your device or password manager. Keep access to it and check how to recover it if you lose your device. Creating a new wallet will not restore the assets in an existing one.

## Choose an asset

Use the token selector at the top of Trade to choose what to buy or sell. Orders are priced in USDC on the NEAR network. The assets and networks available for deposits may differ from those available for trading.

## Unlock your balances

If asked, confirm your passkey through {DYA_BRAND.name} ID to view your confidential balances. You may need to confirm again later, even if you are still signed in.
