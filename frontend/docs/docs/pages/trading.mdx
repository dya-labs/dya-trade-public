# Trading

## Prices on the chart

The chart shows prices from another market, such as NEAR/USDC or NEAR/USDT. These help you follow the market, but they may differ from the price you get on {DYA_BRAND.name}.

Before approving a market order, review the offered price, minimum amount you will receive, and allowed price change. If the offer expires, get a new one before continuing.

## Market orders

Select **Market**, choose Buy or Sell, enter an amount, and review your order. When a current price is available, you can enter the amount in either the token you are trading or the currency used to pay for it.

**Slippage** is how much the price may change before your order completes. Your chosen limit sets the price change you allow; it does not guarantee the order will complete.

## Limit orders

Select **Limit**, set your price in USDC, enter an amount, and choose when the order should expire. Review the details and approve it with your passkey.

**Last** fills in the latest available public trade price, converted to USDC if needed. It may be unavailable when the price data is out of date.

An order may complete all at once, complete in parts, expire, or be cancelled. Setting a limit price does not guarantee a trade.

Wait for cancellation to be confirmed. Funds may take longer to return after an order stops trading.

Your confirmed open orders appear on the selected token's chart as dashed lines. Each line shows the amount left and your limit price. If the chart uses a different currency, {DYA_BRAND.name} needs a current exchange rate to place the line correctly.

## History and fees

Use **Open orders**, **Trade history**, and **Order history** to check your activity. Completed trade details show the amounts you actually received. These may differ from the chart price or an earlier estimate.

Review the costs and amounts before approving each order. Fees and prices can change, so a previous offer may no longer apply.

## If an order's status is unclear

Check the existing order before approving another one. A timeout or connection error does not necessarily mean your order failed.
